# Companion Auto Summon monetization review

Reviewed **28 September 2026**, using the primary sources linked below. This
records published platform rules, remaining publisher questions and an intended
release model. It is not approval from Nexus Mods or Hello Games. No permission
request has been sent, account configured, payment destination chosen or
financial UI implemented as part of this review.

## Published rules and their limits

| Source and published date | Relevant finding |
|---|---|
| [Nexus Donation Options & Guidelines](https://help.nexusmods.com/article/77-donation-options-guidelines), 31 October 2024 | Allows PayPal and off-site donation links on mod, profile and collection pages. Donations must be voluntary thanks. Paid files, updates, assistance, commissions, early access and promises of development in exchange are prohibited. It prohibits excessive reminders inside files; that does not expressly approve an occasional local popup. Publisher-endorsed paid-mod exceptions are not evidence of an NMS arrangement. |
| [Donation Points System Rules](https://help.nexusmods.com/article/68-donation-points-system-terms-of-service), 14 September 2026 | Requires an adult author, published mod and opt-in. Files must be original work or use assets cleared for commercialization or explicitly permitted for DP. No blanket AI exclusion appears in the current rules. This is not automatic CAS eligibility: provenance and permissions still matter, and Nexus controls admission and rewards. Manipulative splitting, update spam and other abuse are forbidden. |
| [File Submission Guidelines](https://help.nexusmods.com/article/28-file-submission-guidelines), 4 September 2026 | AI-generated code, UI and translations belong under **AI-Generated Content**; generated promotional material belongs under **AI Media**. **AI Assisted** requires limited AI involvement and evidence of human-led development and expertise. The rules also restrict advertising, adware and nonessential network communication, and require compliance with the game's agreements. |
| [Official AI categorization announcement](https://forums.nexusmods.com/topic/13542525-an-update-on-generative-ai-categorisation-tagging/), 29 July 2026 | Confirms the distinctions and moderation of misleading tags. CAS should disclose its substantial generated content rather than claim limited assistance. Version history supports transparency; it does not turn substantial generation into limited involvement. |
| [Hello Games EULA](https://www.nomanssky.com/end-user-licence-agreement/), 25 January 2019 | Sections 4.2.4 and 5.2 restrict commercial use. Section 6.2 prohibits uploaded promotional/solicitation content and third-party links in its community context. No explicit exemption for local financial UI or a donated third-party mod was found. Applying those clauses to the proposed local interface remains unresolved; this is not a conclusion that every local link is prohibited. |

Nexus page-level permissions cannot grant rights on behalf of Hello Games.
Replacing a popup with a launcher link does not itself resolve that distinction.
Third-party dependencies and any future bundled runtime require their own rights
review before opting the final package into DP.

## Maximum defensible release plan

1. Release the complete functional mod free, with the same features, updates
   and assistance available regardless of payment.
2. Use Nexus's built-in donation option and a restrained page-level link for
   voluntary one-off or recurring thanks. Do not offer rewards, priority
   support, access privileges or funding targets that promise future mod work.
3. Consider DP only after checking the final package's provenance, licenses and
   eligibility. Disclose AI accurately; do not infer approval from the absence
   of a blanket AI exclusion.
4. Seek a specific answer before implementing the optional local Support/About
   proposal below. No startup ads, unsolicited reminders, automatic browser
   opening or promotion to other multiplayer users is included in the proposal.

The [DP FAQ](https://help.nexusmods.com/article/112-donation-points-faq), checked
28 September 2026, states 1,000 DP equals USD 1 and describes NET90 distribution.
Allocation metrics are private and receipts vary. Neither download counts nor
opt-in establish an earnings forecast.

For discoverability, prioritize clear requirements, a reliable installation,
truthful images and meaningful maintenance. These are supported by Nexus's
[Best Practices for Mod Authors](https://help.nexusmods.com/article/136-best-practices-for-mod-authors)
(5 August 2024); they are not a promised revenue formula.

>

> There would be no startup popup, reminders, automatic network requests,
> multiplayer messages or payment-dependent features, access or assistance.
> One-off and recurring donations would provide no rewards or future-work promise.
>
> We have not implemented this financial interface. Please identify any required
> wording, placement or restrictions. Publisher permission is a separate inquiry.
